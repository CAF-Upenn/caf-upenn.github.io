Who We Are

CAF is part of the new Center for Climate Adaptation and Resilience (CCAR) at Penn's School of Social Policy & Practice, led by Dr. Jisung Park. While reducing emissions is essential, we focus on what comes next — building evidence for how communities, institutions, and economies adapt to the climate shocks already underway.
