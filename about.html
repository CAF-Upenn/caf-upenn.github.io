We are a research community at Penn's School of Social Policy & Practice, generating evidence and ideas to help communities, institutions, and economies adapt to the climate shocks already underway.

Our Mission

Why climate adaptation?

Climate change encompasses more than catastrophic events like severe wildfires, melting polar ice, or rising sea levels. It also affects health, productivity, economic stability, and personal well-being — often in ways that are less visible but no less consequential.

The devastating wildfires in Los Angeles, which affected people from all socioeconomic backgrounds, are a reminder that nobody is immune. While reducing emissions is essential, we are not nearly as prepared as we need to be for the climate shocks that are already in store.

CAF brings together researchers from economics, sociology, demography, and public policy to produce rigorous, actionable evidence on how individuals, communities, and institutions can better adapt to a changing climate.

The Center for Climate Adaptation and Resilience

CAF operates within CCAR — the Center for Climate Adaptation and Resilience — a key initiative at Penn's School of Social Policy & Practice. CCAR advances SP2's mission to drive social change through research and policy, focusing on two major areas of work.

The first is advancing the science of climate adaptation — digging deeper into what makes a community, institution, or economy resilient to climate change. The second is turning research into practical use, providing decision-makers with evidence-based strategies for climate resilience.

"Universities like Penn can — and must — generate actionable insights, research, and leadership to meet this challenge."

— Jisung Park, Director of CCAR

CCAR aligns with the University's larger focus on finding solutions for the most pressing issues of our time, bridging climate, health, and democracy as part of Penn's strategic framework In Principle & Practice.
