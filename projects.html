Heat and Homelessness Mortality

Climate Health Vulnerable Populations Urban Policy

This project examines how extreme heat affects mortality among homeless populations in U.S. cities. We examine whether Code Red and Code Blue policies can protect vulnerable communities during extreme temperature waves.

The goal is to inform policy interventions that better protect homeless individuals from climate risks.
