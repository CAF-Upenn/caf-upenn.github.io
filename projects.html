Heat and Homelessness Mortality

Climate Health Vulnerable Populations Urban Policy

This research examines the relationship between extreme heat exposure and mortality among homeless populations across 20 U.S. counties from 2011-2020. Using HUD Continuum of Care funding data as an instrumental variable for shelter capacity, we investigate how adaptation infrastructure can mitigate climate-related health risks for society's most vulnerable populations.

The study employs innovative data extraction techniques from PDF sources and advanced statistical modeling to quantify the protective effects of shelter access during heat waves. Our findings inform targeted policy interventions for climate adaptation in urban environments.
